Adapting to a new scam

The model was trained on 5 known scam types. A new one appears: fake investment. Here is what happens before and after the system adapts.

1
MissThe old model has never seen this scam and lets it through.
2
DetectA novelty detector, trained only on normal behaviour, flags payments that look unlike normal.
3
LabelAn analyst reviews the most unusual alerts and labels them. Here the analyst is simulated with the true labels.
4
LearnThe model retrains with those few labels and now knows the scam by name.
